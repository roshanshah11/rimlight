# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- A light show around the edges of the screen, played from the music: club, rap and ambiance styles picked per song
  from its first bars, with colors from the album art.
- Works with Spotify, Apple Music and any other app that plays sound.
- Timing: the light is drawn for the moment each frame reaches the screen, minus the output's delay. Calibration
  times speakers and Bluetooth headphones with chirps on the Mac's built-in mic, and with the song itself.
- The optional Spotify extension (through Spicetify) plans the show from Spotify's beat map, sections and synced
  lyrics.
- Welcome window, open at login, a daily update check against GitHub releases, and a `rimlight` command line.
- Universal (Apple silicon and Intel), macOS 15 or later.
